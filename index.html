Economic Indicators

Updated Nov 03, 2026 at 17:19 PST

This site attempts to objectively answer - the question "Are we better off today than in October 2024?"
It does so by comparing today's values of key economic indicators from trusted sources (mostly government agencies) to that of the baseline period, Oct 2023–Oct 2024.
Indicators are grouped by the economic actors for which they are relevant: e.g. consumers (mortgage rates, employment, hourly wages, ...), businesses (investment, production, interest rates, ...), government (spending, national debt service, ...), etc.

Note that the meaning of indicators depends on the context of the economic actor. For example, a rise in wages is good for consumers, but not necessarily for businesses.
