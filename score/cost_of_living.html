What each score item means

  • Inflation: Headline CPI, year-over-year %. Scores 100 at the 2% target and falls faster below target than above it. Curve: target 2, sigma 2.5 above and 1.5 below. Series: CPIAUCSL
  • Gasoline: Real price of regular gasoline. Kept despite being inside CPI, because frequently seen prices shape perceptions beyond their budget share. Curve: percentile over Jan 1990 to Dec 2025, lower better. Series: GASREGW, CPIAUCSL
  • Mortgage payment burden: Monthly principal and interest on 80% of the trailing 4-quarter average of the median new house price, as % of monthly median household income. Income is interpolated between annual values and carried forward with the growth of the 12-month average of per-capita disposable income. Midpoint 25%, below the Atlanta Fed's 30%, which includes taxes and insurance. Replaces the raw mortgage rate. Curve: threshold at 25, steepness 4, lower better. Series: MORTGAGE30US, MSPUS, MEHOINUSA646N, A229RC0
  • Groceries: CPI food at home, year-over-year %. Curve: target 2, sigma 2.5 above and 1.5 below. Series: CUSR0000SAF11
