What each score item means

  • Real income excluding transfers: Per-capita real personal income excluding transfers, year-over-year % change. Curve: percentile over Jan 1990 to Dec 2025, higher better. Series: W875RX1, POPTHM
  • Real wages: Employment Cost Index for wages, deflated by CPI. Replaces average hourly earnings, which rise in recessions as low-wage jobs disappear. Curve: percentile over Jan 1990 to Dec 2025, higher better. Series: ECIWAG, CPIAUCSL
  • Real spending: Per-capita real personal consumption expenditures, year-over-year % change. Curve: percentile over Jan 1990 to Dec 2025, higher better. Series: PCEC96, POPTHM
