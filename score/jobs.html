What each score item means

  • Unemployment rate: 3-month average of the unemployment rate. Curve: percentile over Jan 1990 to Dec 2025, lower better. Series: UNRATE
  • Prime-age employment rate: Employment-population ratio, ages 25-54. Added to cover participation, which the unemployment rate misses. Curve: percentile over Jan 1990 to Dec 2025, higher better. Series: LNS12300060
  • Quits rate: Cross-listed from business. Workers quit when they are confident of finding a better job. Curve: percentile over Jan 1990 to Dec 2025, higher better. Series: JTSQUR
  • Initial claims: 4-week average of initial claims divided by nonfarm payrolls, so the series is comparable as employment grows. Curve: percentile over Jan 1990 to Dec 2025, lower better. Series: IC4WSA, PAYEMS
