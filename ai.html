8 AI stocks represent more than 40% of S&P 500

8 AI-driven stocks represent almost 40% of the market capitalization of the S&P 500, and almost 50% of the gains the index made since October 2024.

The 8 stocks are: NVDA, AAPL, GOOG, MSFT,AMZN, META, AVGO, TSLA

What each indicator means

  • NVIDIA Stock Price: Sentiment gauge for a single company; read alongside ai_sox_index.
    Reference: [1]
  • Percentage of the S&P 500 Market Capitalization that is AI-related stocks: Percentage of the S&P 500 Market Capitalization that is attributable to AI-related stocks
    Converted to the combined market cap of NVDA, AAPL, GOOG, MSFT, AMZN, META, AVGO, TSLA as a percentage of the S&P 500's.
    References: [2], [3], [4], [5], [6], [7], [8], [9], [10]
  • Percentage of the S&P 500 Returns that is AI-related stocks: Percentage of the S&P 500 Returns that is attributable to AI-related stocks
    Converted to the dollar gain in the combined market cap of NVDA, AAPL, GOOG, MSFT, AMZN, META, AVGO, TSLA since baseline start, as a percentage of the S&P 500's gain.
    References: [2], [3], [4], [5], [6], [7], [8], [9], [10]
  • PHLX Semiconductor Index: 30 largest U.S.-traded companies primarily involved in the design, distribution, manufacture, and sale of semiconductors.
    Reference: [11]
  • Hyperscaler Stock Index: Hyperscaler stock index composed of major tech companies like Google. Daily stock prices in USD.
    Averaged with AMZN, MSFT into an equal-weighted index; each stock is scaled to its peak price in the baseline window.
    References: [12], [13], [14]
  • TSMC Stock Price: Chipmakers stock index composed of major chipmaking companies like TSMC. Daily stock prices in USD.
    Averaged with 000660.KS, MU into an equal-weighted index; each stock is scaled to its peak price in the baseline window.
    References: [15], [16], [17]
  • Generative AI adoption outside work, adults 18-64: Real-Time Population Survey GenAI Adoption Tracker. History from Aug 2024.
    Reference: [18]
  • Businesses using AI in the past month (Census BTOS): This is a survey-based measure from the Census BTOS.
    Reference: [19]
  • Businesses paying for AI tools (Ramp AI Index): Card and bill-pay data from Ramp customers, which skew tech-forward and VC-backed. Paid adoption only; reads well above Census by construction.
    Reference: [20]
  • Construction spending: power: Generation and grid build, the other physical bottleneck alongside data centers.
    Adjusted for inflation with price index PPIACO, in today's prices; then smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    References: [21], [22]
  • CPI, electricity: Household cost side of the data center build.
    Converted to the 12-month percent change.
    Reference: [23]
  • Real average hourly earnings: electrical contractors: Industry (NAICS 23821), not occupation: occupational wages (OEWS) are annual. From the BLS API: FRED does not carry NAICS 238210 detail.
    Adjusted for inflation with price index CPIAUCSL, in today's prices.
    References: [24], [25]
  • Employment: electrical contractors: Likely a better tightness signal than wages, which adjust slowly. From the BLS API: FRED does not carry NAICS 238210 detail.
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [26]
  • Construction job postings (Indeed): Closest proxy for electrician openings: no occupation-level openings series exists. Indeed requires permission for republication.
    Reference: [27]
  • Electrical engineering job postings (Indeed): Indeed requires permission for republication.
    Reference: [28]
  • Employment: computing infrastructure, data processing and hosting: NAICS 518.
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [29]
