Produce Price Index

The 3 measures of Producer Price Index illustrate how businesses have had to compress their margins. Costs for raw (unprocessed) and intermediate (processed) goods have risen by double digit figures, while prices final goods (sold to their businesss or consumer customers) have risen by only 5.6%.

What each indicator means

  • Core capital goods new orders: Manufacturers' New Orders: Nondefense Capital Goods Excluding Aircraft
    Adjusted for inflation with price index PPIACO, in today's prices.
    References: [1], [2]
  • Manufacturing new orders, total: Manufacturers' New Orders: Total Manufacturing
    Adjusted for inflation with price index PPIACO, in today's prices.
    References: [3], [2]
  • Inventories-to-sales ratio: Best early recession tell here: inventories rising into flat sales precedes production cuts.
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [4]
  • Industrial production: Measures the real output of all relevant establishments located in the United States, regardless of their ownership
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [5]
  • Capacity utilization: Covers manufacturing, mining, and electric and gas utilities
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [6]
  • Employment Cost Index: Wages & Salaries: Employment Cost Index: Wages and Salaries: Private Industry Workers
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [7]
  • Job openings rate (Nonfarm): Job Openings: Total Nonfarm
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [8]
  • Layoffs & discharges: Total Nonfarm: Layoffs & discharges: Total Nonfarm
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [9]
  • Business applications (EINs): Business Applications: Total for All North American Industries in the United States
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [10]
  • Diesel price: Impacts businesses: freight, agriculture and construction rather than consumers.
    Reference: [11]
  • WTI crude oil: West Texas Intermediate crude oil spot price.
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [12]
  • Global price of Copper: The best single commodity signal of global industrial demand
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [13]
  • PPI unprocessed goods for intermediate demand: Most upstream rung. Noisiest series in this bucket -- BLS one-month changes run to +/-6 to 14% (Apr 2020: -14.3%), since unprocessed commodities have no fabrication stage to damp them. Hence the smoothing.
    Converted to the 12-month percent change; then smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [14]
  • PPI processed goods for intermediate demand: Midstream rung, and the input leg of the margin spread. Fabricated inputs outrunning output prices means firms are absorbing cost, which lands in earnings a quarter or two later.
    Converted to the 12-month percent change; then smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [15]
  • PPI final demand: Headline business inflation. Prices received by domestic producers -- seller's perspective, excludes imports, no sales tax.
    Converted to the 12-month percent change; then smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [16]
  • 10-Year Treasury Yield Averaged Daily: Market Yield on U.S. Treasury Securities at 10-Year Constant Maturity, Quoted on an Investment Basis
    Reference: [17]
  • Commercial & industrial loans: Commercial & industrial loans, all commercial banks.
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [18]
  • Nonfinancial corporate debt: Nonfinancial corporate debt
    Reference: [19]
