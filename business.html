Shaded green when the indicator moved in its good direction and red when it moved the other way; neutral indicators and unchanged values are not shaded.

Bold when larger than 10 (in either direction) and measured in % or % pts, to flag the biggest moves.
How the Delta is measured: % is a percent change, used for levels, prices and indexes; % pts (percentage points) is a simple difference, used for series that are already rates, such as an unemployment rate; Δ is a simple difference in the series' own units, used for index readings where a percent change would mislead.
Which way is good news: rising means a rise is an improvement, falling means a fall is an improvement, and neutral means the move is shown but not judged.

What each indicator means

  • 10-Year Treasury Yield Averaged Daily: Market Yield on U.S. Treasury Securities at 10-Year Constant Maturity, Quoted on an Investment Basis
    Reference: [1]
  • Business applications (EINs): Business Applications: Total for All NAICS in the United States
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [2]
  • Capacity utilization: Covers manufacturing, mining, and electric and gas utilities
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [3]
  • Commercial & industrial loans: Commercial & industrial loans, all commercial banks.
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [4]
  • Core capital goods new orders: Manufacturers' New Orders: Nondefense Capital Goods Excluding Aircraft
    Adjusted for inflation with price index PPIACO, in today's prices.
    References: [5], [6]
  • Diesel price: Prices freight, agriculture and construction rather than commuting.
    Reference: [7]
  • Employment Cost Index: Wages & Salaries: Employment Cost Index: Wages and Salaries: Private Industry Workers
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [8]
  • Global price of Copper: The best single commodity signal of global industrial demand
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [9]
  • Industrial production: Measures the real output of all relevant establishments located in the United States, regardless of their ownership, but not those located in U.S. territories
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [10]
  • Inventories-to-sales ratio: Best early recession tell here: inventories rising into flat sales precedes production cuts.
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [11]
  • Job openings rate (Nonfarm): Job Openings: Total Nonfarm
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [12]
  • Layoffs & discharges: Total Nonfarm: Layoffs & discharges: Total Nonfarm
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [13]
  • Manufacturing new orders, total: Manufacturers' New Orders: Total Manufacturing
    Adjusted for inflation with price index PPIACO, in today's prices.
    References: [14], [6]
  • Nonfinancial corporate debt: Nonfinancial corporate debt
    Reference: [15]
  • Producer Price Index: Producer Price Index for final demand.
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [16]
  • WTI crude oil: West Texas Intermediate crude oil spot price.
    Smoothed with a trailing 3-month average.
    Reference: [17]
